Audio Assets Credits & Sources
===============================

All audio assets in Assets/Audio/ were procedurally generated by custom Python synthesis script (wave, struct, math) for CS464 Assignment 04.

Files:
- pickup.wav: Short rising two-tone blip (0.2s, 44.1 kHz 16-bit mono PCM).
- hit.wav: Short noise burst with exponential decay and low thump (0.2s, 44.1 kHz 16-bit mono PCM).
- click.wav: Short tick (0.05s, 44.1 kHz 16-bit mono PCM).
- win.wav: Rising arpeggio chord progression (1.0s, 44.1 kHz 16-bit mono PCM).
- lose.wav: Falling pitch sweep tone (1.0s, 44.1 kHz 16-bit mono PCM).
- music_loop.wav: 8-second ambient chord pad and arpeggio loop (44.1 kHz 16-bit mono PCM).
- hum_loop.wav: 110 Hz tone with 2 Hz vibrato, exact 220 full cycles for clean looping (2.0s, 44.1 kHz 16-bit mono PCM).
